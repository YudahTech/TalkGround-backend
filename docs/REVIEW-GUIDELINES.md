# TalkGround — Review Guidelines

**For all users posting reviews on TalkGround.**

---

## Our standard

TalkGround only works when reviews are **honest, specific, and respectful**. These guidelines apply to every review, photo, and receipt upload.

---

## Do

- Write about **your own experience** at the business
- Include **specific details** — dish names, prices, wait times, room quality, staff behaviour, neighbourhood context
- Be **balanced** — mention both positives and negatives when applicable
- **Upload a receipt** if you want a "Verified visit" badge (optional; receipt stays private)
- **Update your review** if your experience changes significantly
- **Report** reviews you believe are fake (use the Report button)

---

## Don't

- Post reviews for places you have **not visited**
- Post **fake** or **paid** reviews (including reviews in exchange for discounts without disclosure)
- Use **hate speech**, slurs, or personal attacks on staff by name
- Post **defamatory** statements presented as fact (opinions are fine; false factual claims are not)
- **Coordinate** with others to inflate or deflate ratings
- Create **multiple accounts** to post additional reviews
- Upload receipt images belonging to **other people**
- Share **private business information** unrelated to a consumer experience

---

## Rating guidance

- **1–2 stars:** Acceptable for genuinely bad experiences. Explain specifically what went wrong.
- **3 stars:** Fine for average experiences.
- **4–5 stars:** Great for standout experiences. Say what made it special.

We do not remove negative reviews simply because a business dislikes them. We remove reviews that violate these guidelines.

---

## Photos

- Upload photos of the **food, venue, or room** — not other customers without consent
- Do not upload offensive or irrelevant images
- Maximum 3 photos per review

---

## Receipt verification

- Receipt upload is **optional**
- Your receipt is reviewed by our team and **never shown publicly**
- A "Verified visit" badge means we confirmed the receipt matches the business
- Rejected receipts do not affect your review — it stays published without the badge

---

## Consequences

| Violation | Action |
|---|---|
| First minor violation | Review removed + warning |
| Fake/spam review | Review removed + account warning |
| Repeat violations | Account suspension |
| Coordinated attack | All involved accounts suspended |
| Illegal content | Immediate removal + possible legal report |

---

## Appeals

If your review was removed and you believe this was an error, contact: moderation@talkground.ng

Include your username, the business name, and why you believe the review was compliant.

We aim to respond within 48 hours.

---

## For businesses

TalkGround does not currently offer business account responses. Do not incentivise customers to post reviews on TalkGround. Doing so violates these guidelines and may result in removal of affected reviews.
