# Revyu — Validation Survey Instrument

**Purpose:** B2B validation & problem quantification  
**Route:** `/survey`  
**Completion time:** 3–4 minutes  
**Incentive:** None

---

## Screening

**S1.** Are you 18 years of age or older?  
- Yes → Continue | No → End

**S2.** Have you lived in or visited Lagos or Abuja in the past 12 months?  
- Yes, I live there | Yes, I visited → Continue | No → End

---

## Q1. Discovery behaviour

When you need to choose a restaurant or place to stay in Lagos/Abuja, what do you typically do first? *(Select up to 2)*

- Ask friends or colleagues
- Search Google Maps
- Check Instagram or TikTok
- Check Twitter/X
- Use a review website or app
- Visit the business website directly
- Other

---

## Q2. Problem severity

How difficult is it to find trustworthy information about restaurants and hotels in Nigeria?

`1 — Not difficult` → `5 — Very difficult`

---

## Q3. Negative experience

In the past 12 months, have you had an experience where a restaurant or hotel was significantly worse than what you expected based on what you read or heard online?

- Yes, more than once
- Yes, once
- No
- Not sure

---

## Q4. Behaviour change *(if Q3 = yes)*

How did that experience affect your behaviour? *(Select all)*

- I now research more carefully
- I rely more on personal recommendations
- I have lost trust in online reviews
- I complained or left a negative review
- It didn't change my behaviour

---

## Q5. Trust baseline

How much do you trust online reviews of Nigerian businesses today?

`1 — Don't trust at all` → `5 — Trust completely`

---

## Q6. Trust drivers

Which would most increase your trust? *(Rank top 3)*

- Phone-verified reviewer
- Photo of place/food
- Proof of visit (receipt)
- Platform removes fakes quickly
- Specific pros and cons
- Large number of reviews

---

## Q7. Verified visit badge

Would a "Verified Visit" badge (receipt reviewed by platform) increase your trust?

- Much more likely
- Somewhat more likely
- No difference
- Somewhat less likely
- Much less likely

---

## Q8. Receipt willingness

Would you upload a receipt to prove your visit?

- Yes, if receipt details are kept private
- Yes, even if some info visible
- Maybe
- No

---

## Q9. Eat out frequency

How often do you eat at restaurants or order from restaurants?

- Daily | Several times/week | Once/week | Few times/month | Rarely

---

## Q10. Hotel frequency

How often do you book hotels, guest houses, or short-lets in Lagos/Abuja?

- Weekly | Monthly | Few times/year | Rarely | Never

---

## Q11. Review history

Have you ever written an online review for a Nigerian business?

- Yes, Google Maps or similar
- Yes, social media
- Yes, Nigerian review platform
- No, but I would consider it
- No, and I wouldn't

---

## Q12. Platform demand

If a trusted platform existed, how likely would you use it before deciding?

`1 — Very unlikely` → `5 — Very likely`

---

## Q13. Write willingness

How likely would you write a review after a notable experience?

`1 — Very unlikely` → `5 — Very likely`
