## 1. Who we are

TalkGround is a review platform for restaurants and hotels in Lagos and Abuja. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

**Data Controller:** [COMPANY NAME LTD], [registered address]

---

## 2. Data we collect

### Account data
- Email address
- Phone number
- Display name and username
- Password (stored as encrypted hash — never plain text)

### Review data
- Review text, ratings, photos you upload
- Receipt images (if you choose to upload — see Section 5)

### Technical data
- IP address hash (not raw IP — used for fraud prevention)
- Browser type, device information (via analytics, if enabled)
- Cookies for authentication session

### Waitlist and survey data
- Name, email, city, preferences
- Survey responses

## 4. How we use your data

- Provide and improve the Platform
- Verify your identity (phone OTP)
- Display your reviews and public profile
- Prevent spam and fake reviews
- Notify waitlist members about launch
- Respond to support and legal requests

We do **not** sell your personal data.

---

## 5. Receipt images — special handling

If you upload a receipt for verification:

- The receipt image is stored securely on Cloudinary
- It is **never displayed publicly** on the Platform
- Only authorised administrators can view it for verification
- If verified, only a "Verified visit" badge is shown — not receipt contents
- Receipt images are deleted upon account deletion request

## 7. Data retention

| Data type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account data | Until account deletion request |
| Reviews | Until you delete them or account is removed |
| Receipt images | Until verification decision + 90 days, or deletion request |
| Survey responses | 2 years (anonymised for analysis after 1 year) |
| Waitlist entries | Until launch + 12 months |
| IP hashes | 12 months |

## 8. Your rights (NDPR)

You have the right to:

- **Access** your personal data
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your data (right to erasure)
- **Withdraw consent** for waitlist/survey communications
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Lodge a complaint** with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)

To exercise rights: privacy@talkground.ng — we respond within 30 days.

---

## 9. Security

- Passwords hashed with bcrypt
- HTTPS on all connections
- JWT tokens for authentication
- Admin access restricted and logged
- Raw IP addresses not stored
