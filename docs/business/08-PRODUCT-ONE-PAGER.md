## The problem you know

You've been there. A restaurant looked amazing on Instagram. The hotel had great photos. You went — and it was nothing like what you expected.

In Nigeria, there's no place you can **actually trust** for honest restaurant and hotel reviews.

---

## What TalkGround does

TalkGround is a review platform built for Nigerians.

**Three simple steps:**

1. **Search** — Find restaurants and hotels in Lagos or Abuja
2. **Read** — See honest reviews from real people
3. **Review** — Share your experience and help the next person

---

## Why TalkGround reviews are different

| Feature | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Verified visit badge** | Reviewer uploaded a receipt — we confirmed they were actually there |
| **Phone-verified users** | Real accounts, not anonymous bots |
| **Moderated reviews** | Fake and spam reviews get removed |
| **Photos** | See the actual food and venue |
| **Pros & cons** | Balanced, structured feedback — not just a star rating |

**Your receipt is never shown publicly.** Only the badge.

---

## Who is TalkGround for?

- **New to Lagos or Abuja?** Find where to eat and stay — fast.
- **Eat out every week?** Stop gambling on random picks.
- **Just had an amazing (or terrible) meal?** Warn others or spread the word.
- **Booking a short-let?** Read what previous guests actually experienced.
