## Slide 2 — The problem

**Nigerians can't trust what they read online about local businesses.**

- Google Maps: thin coverage for bukas, new spots, local hotels
- Social media: hype, not structure
- Nigerian review sites: exist but no city won on trust
- Result: bad meals, bad stays, wasted money — every week

> "I spent three hours on Instagram and still picked the wrong restaurant."

## Slide 4 — The solution

**TalkGround: Search → Read → Review**

A review platform built for trust, not volume.

- Find restaurants and hotels in your city
- Read reviews with verification signals
- Write your own — help the next person

**Live product:** Full MVP built (web app + API + moderation)

## Slide 5 — Trust moat

**Why people will believe TalkGround reviews**

| Layer | How |
|---|---|
| Verified visits | Receipt upload → admin verified → badge (receipt private) |
| Account integrity | Phone OTP + email before first review |
| Anti-spam | IP flagging, pattern detection, velocity alerts |
| Human moderation | Founder queue, 48h SLA |

*No competitor in Nigeria leads with receipt verification.*
