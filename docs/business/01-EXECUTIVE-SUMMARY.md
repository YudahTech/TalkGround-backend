## One sentence

Revyu is where Nigerians find trusted reviews for restaurants and hotels in Lagos and Abuja — starting with receipt-verified reviews no competitor offers at local depth.

---

## The problem

Every week, millions of Nigerians ask the same question: *"Where should I eat?"* or *"Where should I stay?"*

They check Google Maps (thin local coverage), scroll Twitter (unstructured hype), or ask WhatsApp groups (limited reach). Existing Nigerian review platforms exist but have failed to build **density + trust** in any single city.

**The trust gap is the product.** Without it, a review platform is just an empty directory.

---

## The solution

Revyu is a review platform built for one job: help people **find, read, and write** trustworthy reviews for restaurants and hotels.

**Core loop:** Search → Read → Review

**Trust stack (our moat):**
- Receipt-verified "Verified visit" badges (receipt kept private)
- Phone + email verification before reviewing
- Automated anti-spam (IP, pattern, velocity flagging)
- Founder-led moderation (48-hour SLA)
