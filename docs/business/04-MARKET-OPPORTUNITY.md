# Revyu — Market Opportunity Brief

**Confidential — June 2026**

---

## 1. Executive summary

Revyu addresses a **trust deficit** in how Nigerians discover restaurants and hotels — a high-frequency, high-emotion decision happening millions of times per month in Lagos and Abuja alone.

The opportunity is not "create another review app." It is to become the **default trust layer** for local dining and hospitality in Nigeria's two largest urban markets — then expand.

---

## 2. Problem validation

### Consumer pain points

| Pain | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Can't find trusted local reviews | Users default to Twitter, WhatsApp, Google Maps |
| Misleading online hype | "Instagram vs reality" is a cultural meme |
| No verification signal | Cannot tell if reviewer actually visited |
| Fragmented information | No single searchable source for Lagos/Abuja |

### Survey instrument (live at revyu.ng/survey)

Key questions designed to quantify:
- Problem severity (Q2: difficulty finding trustworthy info)
- Trust gap (Q5: trust in Nigerian online reviews)
- Receipt badge acceptance (Q7)
- Platform demand (Q12)
- Write willingness (Q13)

**Target:** 300+ responses for investor-grade validation data.

## 3. Target market

### Primary cities (Phase 1)

**Lagos**
- Population: 15M+ metropolitan
- Characteristics: High eat-out culture, dense restaurant scene, large newcomer population (NYSC, relocations, travel)
- Areas of focus: VI, Lekki, Ikeja, Yaba, Surulere

**Abuja**
- Population: 3M+ metropolitan
- Characteristics: Government/corporate relocations, hotel demand, growing restaurant scene
- Areas of focus: Garki, Wuse, Maitama, CBD

### Target personas

| Persona | Size (est.) | Frequency | Willingness to review |
|---|---|---|---|
| Young professional (22–35) | Large | Eat out 2–4x/week | Medium–High |
| Newcomer / NYSC | Medium | High need, low local knowledge | Medium |
| Business traveller | Medium | Hotel bookings monthly | Low–Medium |
| Contributor / community member | Small but critical | Variable | High |

## 5. Why existing solutions fail

| Solution | Coverage | Trust | Structure | Local depth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google Maps | Medium | Low–Medium | High | Low for new/local spots |
| TripAdvisor | Low (NG) | Medium | High | Hotels only, thin |
| Ratecrest / Krediview | Low | Low | High | No verification |
| Twitter / Instagram | High | Very Low | None | None |
| WhatsApp groups | Medium | Medium | None | Not searchable |

**Gap Revyu fills:** Structured + searchable + verified + city-dense.

---

## 6. Market trends supporting Revyu

1. **Rising eat-out culture** — urban professionals time-poor, dining out default
2. **Short-let explosion** — Airbnb-style stays need review layer beyond photos
3. **Digital-native consumers** — under-35 expects review platforms to exist
4. **Trust crisis in social media** — influencer fatigue creates opening for verified UGC
5. **Local business digitisation** — restaurants/hotels increasingly findable online but not reviewable

## 10. Conclusion

The market opportunity is not creating demand for reviews — Nigerians already seek them constantly. The opportunity is **owning trust** in two cities first, then scaling what works.

**Revyu is positioned to be that trust layer.**
