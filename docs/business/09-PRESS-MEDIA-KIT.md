---

## Company boilerplate (long)

**Revyu** is building Nigeria's most trusted review platform for restaurants and hotels. Founded in 2026, Revyu addresses a gap in how Nigerians discover where to eat and stay — existing tools like Google Maps lack local depth, while social media offers hype without structure or accountability.

Revyu's trust model includes receipt-verified reviews (where reviewers upload proof of visit, kept private, with only a "Verified visit" badge shown publicly), phone and email verification, automated anti-spam detection, and human moderation with a 48-hour response SLA.

The platform launches in Lagos and Abuja on 1 August 2026, focusing initially on restaurants and hotels before expanding to additional categories and cities.
