## Problem

Nigerians lack a **trusted, locally dense** platform for restaurant and hotel reviews. Global tools have shallow coverage. Nigerian alternatives haven't won on verification or city-level depth. Consumers default to unstructured social media — and frequently get burned.

---

## Solution

Revyu: **Search → Read trusted reviews → Write your own.**

Built-in trust: receipt-verified badges, phone verification, anti-spam flagging, human moderation.
