# TalkGround — Go-to-Market Strategy

**Confidential — June 2026**  
**Launch target:** 1 August 2026

---

## 1. GTM thesis

TalkGround wins through **city density**, not nationwide awareness.

**Formula:** Seed supply (brands) → activate demand (waitlist) → prove trust (verification) → monetise (brand subscriptions)

---

## 2. Phase 0 — Validate (June 2026)

### Survey campaign
- **Goal:** 300+ responses proving problem exists
- **Channels:** Twitter/X, Instagram, WhatsApp groups, NYSC groups, founder networks
- **Asset:** revyu.ng/survey
- **Output:** Pitch deck metrics + Survey Results Report

### Waitlist campaign
- **Goal:** 500+ launch-ready users
- **Channels:** Same as survey + link in survey completion screen
- **Asset:** revyu.ng/waitlist
- **Segment by:** City (Lagos/Abuja), primary use (eat out / hotels / write reviews)

### Success criteria
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Survey responses | 300+ |
| % rating problem 4–5/5 (Q2) | ≥60% |
| Waitlist signups | 500+ |
| Lagos/Abuja split | Tracked |

---

## 3. Phase 1 — Seed supply (June–July 2026)

### Brand seeding
- **Goal:** 150–200 brands before soft launch
- **Split:** 100 Lagos, 50 Abuja
- **Sources:** Personal knowledge, Google Maps, Instagram, EatDrinkLagos-style blogs, hotel directories
- **Owner:** Founders (5 brands/day minimum)
- **Tool:** Admin panel at `/admin/brands`

### Initial review supply
- **Goal:** 20–30 authentic founder reviews on flagship brands
- **Rule:** Must be genuine experiences — no fabrication
- **Purpose:** Cold start — no brand should have 0 reviews at launch

## 4. Phase 2 — Soft launch (28–31 July 2026)

### Wave strategy

| Wave | Date | Audience | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 28 Jul | Friends & family | 20–30 |
| 1 | 29 Jul | First 50% waitlist | 250+ |
| 2 | 31 Jul | Remaining waitlist | 250+ |
| 3 | 1 Aug | Public | Open |

### Email templates needed
1. "You're in — TalkGround is live for you" (waitlist)
2. "Review a place you know" (contributor cohort)
3. "Lagos brands now on TalkGround" (Lagos segment)
4. "Abuja brands now on TalkGround" (Abuja segment)

## 5. Phase 3 — Public launch (1 August 2026)

### Launch day activities
- Social media announcement (Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn)
- Founder personal posts with specific brand examples
- Press outreach (tech blogs, lifestyle media — see Press Kit)
- Monitor moderation queue every 4 hours

### Launch week targets
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| New registrations | 100+ |
| Reviews published | 50+ |
| Brands suggested by users | 10+ |
| Moderation SLA | <48h |

|---|---|
| SEO | Brand pages indexed: "{Brand} Reviews Lagos \| TalkGround" |
| WhatsApp | Shareable brand URLs with OG preview cards |
| Word of mouth | "Check TalkGround before you eat" positioning |
| User suggestions | Community fills brand gaps |

### Paid (post-funding)
| Channel | Tactic | Budget priority |
|---|---|---|

| Twitter/X | Problem-aware threads | Medium |
| Google Ads | "restaurants in Lekki reviews" | Medium |

## 7. B2B GTM (brand subscriptions — Q4 2026)

See **B2B Partnership Pitch** document.

**Sequence:**
1. Prove density (200+ brands, 500+ reviews)
2. Identify top-reviewed brands
3. Pitch: "Your customers are already talking about you on TalkGround"
4. Offer free 3-month pilot → convert to subscription
