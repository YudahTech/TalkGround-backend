# TalkGround — Investor One-Pager

**Confidential — June 2026**

---

## The Problem

Nigerians choosing where to eat or stay — especially in Lagos and Abuja — lack a **trusted, locally dense** review platform. Global tools (Google Maps, TripAdvisor) have shallow coverage. Nigerian alternatives (Ratecrest, Krediview, Ethos) exist but haven't achieved critical mass or strong verification.

**Result:** People rely on Twitter and WhatsApp gossip. Bad experiences go unreported structurally. Good businesses don't get the visibility they deserve.

---

## The Solution

**TalkGround** is a review platform built exclusively for Nigerian restaurants and hotels — starting with Lagos and Abuja.

**Core loop:** Search → Read trusted reviews → Write your own.

**Trust moat:**
- Receipt-verified "Verified visit" badges (receipt kept private)
- Phone + email verification
- Automated spam flagging (IP, pattern, velocity)
- Founder-led moderation (48h SLA)
